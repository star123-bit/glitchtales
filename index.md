# GlitchTales

GlitchTales publishes short animated 3D stories (about 60 seconds) with a twist, on TikTok, YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels.

The videos are produced with our own software on our own computer and published to our own accounts through the official APIs of each platform (TikTok Content Posting API, YouTube Data API, Instagram Graph API).
