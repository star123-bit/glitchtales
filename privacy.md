# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 November 2026

Responsible: GlitchTales (contact at the end of this page)

## What GlitchTales is

GlitchTales is our own software. It connects only to our own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts, through the official login (OAuth) of each platform, to upload our videos and read their statistics. Nobody else can sign in to it.

## Data we access

From our own accounts, through the official APIs, we access: basic profile information (account ID, display name), statistics of our own videos (views, likes, comments, shares), public comments on our own videos so we can reply to them, and the publishing status of our uploads.

## How we use and store it

We use this data only to publish our videos, reply to comments on them and learn which videos perform well. Everything is stored locally on our own computer. Access tokens are kept in local files that are never shared or published. We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties; data is only sent to the platform it came from. Comment texts and statistics are deleted when they are no longer needed for this purpose.

## People who comment on our videos

If you comment on one of our videos, we see your public username and comment through the platform, exactly as anyone viewing the video does. We use it only to reply. You can delete your comment on the platform at any time.

## This website

This site is hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not use cookies, analytics or tracking. GitHub may log technical data such as IP addresses; see the GitHub Privacy Statement.

## Revoking access and your rights

Access to an account can be revoked at any time in the security settings of TikTok, Google or Instagram. You can ask us for information about, correction of or deletion of any of your data, using the contact below.
